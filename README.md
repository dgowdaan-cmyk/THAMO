## 🚨 The Problem

Investment scams are increasingly designed around psychological manipulation rather than only technical attacks.

Victims may encounter:

- Guaranteed or unrealistic return promises
- Fake investment experts
- WhatsApp and Telegram trading groups
- Artificial urgency
- Fake SEBI or government impersonation
- Requests for release or withdrawal fees
- OTP and remote-access requests
- Fake investment applications
- Recovery scams after the initial fraud

When people are under pressure, they often struggle to determine what is happening and what action to take.

THAMO addresses this gap by providing a simple safety-oriented workflow from **recognition → understanding → action**.

# 💡 Our Solution

THAMO is built around three critical moments:

### 1. BEFORE PAYMENT

Help users pause before transferring money.

THAMO identifies common warning signs such as:

- Guaranteed returns
- Artificial urgency
- Suspicious payment requests
- Personal UPI/bank-account requests
- Investment group invitations
- Impersonation

---

### 2. UNDER PRESSURE

Help users who are actively being manipulated or threatened.

Examples include:

- Fake officials
- "Digital arrest" threats
- Account blocking threats
- Fake legal action
- Release-fee demands
- Isolation and secrecy tactics

THAMO provides simple, deterministic safety guidance designed to reduce panic.

---

### 3. AFTER SUSPECTED FRAUD

Help users understand their immediate next steps.

THAMO guides users toward:

- Preserving evidence
- Recording transaction information
- Contacting appropriate official channels
- Reporting suspected cyber fraud
- Avoiding secondary recovery scams

---

# 🧠 Core Intelligence

THAMO currently uses a **deterministic rule-based detection engine** for safety-critical scam identification.

Instead of allowing an unrestricted AI model to make safety decisions, the system uses predefined indicators and deterministic policies.

### Detection categories

The engine can identify indicators related to:

- Guaranteed returns
- Urgency
- Release fees
- Recovery fees
- OTP requests
- Remote-access requests
- Impersonation
- Investment group invitations
- Secrecy requests

The system returns the detected phrase along with an explanation of why it is considered a warning sign.
